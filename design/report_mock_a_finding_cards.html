How numbers are computed

Normal is the mean of the 12 prior windows; the band is their minimum to maximum.

z = (current − mean) ÷ max(standard deviation, 2% of the mean). A move is large above |z| 3 and moderate above 2, and only counts when it clears the metric's materiality floor and goes in the bad direction.

Metrics of the same family (for example physical reads and Physical Reads Per Sec) are shown once; the twin is folded into the card.
